Trellis math

The cucumbers are already vining and the hardware store sells slats by the meter. Trellis math counts the slats your grid actually takes at the gap you actually get, measures the twine for the knotted version, and strings the espalier wall with the right rows, anchors and wire. The three tools

  • Slat grid - trellis dimensions become slat counts, the real gap and lumber meters.
  • Twine grid - the same grid becomes line counts and twine meters with knot allowance.
  • Espalier wire - wall dimensions become wire rows, vine eyes and tensioned wire meters.

Labeled norms

Slat grids read well at about 15 cm spacing; the real gap is the width divided between the slats. Twine grids use the same squares with about 10% extra for knots and tie-offs. Espalier wires run about 40 cm apart up the wall, anchored about every 60 cm, with about 5% extra for tensioning. Labeled garden-build references, not rules.
